# Operations Architect Hub

> **Private.** Contains client names, billing details and escalation cases. Do not make this repository public.

The Levrly Operations Architect team's working reference: the client lifecycle, the OPTIMIZE and UNLOCK methods, meetings, SOPs and call scripts, handoff and escalation rules, and the decisions leadership has made in the Escalation Tracker. Built on November 24, 2026 from the 16 documents in the Operations Architect Team Process folder.  
When this repository and a source document disagree, the source document is the authority. Update both when a process changes.

## Plans at a glance

Only Sales quotes prices. Never quote upgrade or add-on prices on an OA call; flag interest to Sales the same day.

|  | Foundation | Build | Scale | Launchpad |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **For** | First VA hire plus systems and training | One VA plus an Architect who meets the client and VA monthly | Two VAs plus weekly Architect hours | The OA team carries one project for 30 days |
| **VA credits** | 1 | 1 | 2 | 0 |
| **OA time** | None (a 20-hour block can be added) | 2 hours a month | 2 hours a week | 20 hours over 30 days |
| **Goal** | 90 days | 90 days | 90 days, 2 weekly habits | 30 days, one blue dot |
| **Documents within 48 hours** | Client Roadmap, VA JD, 30/60/90-Day Success Guide | Same as Foundation | Roadmap, both JDs, 30/60/90 Guide | Client Roadmap, 30-Day Project Plan, Project Scoreboard |
