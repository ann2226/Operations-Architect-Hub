# Handoff and Escalation Rules

Nothing moves forward with missing information. These rules apply to every handoff in every department, and every client or operational issue is logged in the [Levrly Escalation Tracker](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1t2rKmmxya7lKrGfd2sLYZsXH7n5TINuC/view).

## Seven handoff rules

> 1. **Record:** every lead and client is in Levrly OS or GHL.  
> 2. **Send:** email the next owner with the plan, what's done, what's open, links and the next action.  
> 3. **Check:** if details are incomplete, escalate right away. Don't start the next step with gaps.  
> 4. **Accept:** the receiver replies "Accepted" within 1 business day, or lists what is missing.  
> 5. **File:** handoff documents go in Levrly OS under the client profile. Both owners update the Ops Arch Active Clients tracker the same day.  
> 6. **Communicate:** internal chats stay in each department's chat space.  
> 7. **Escalate:** not accepted in 1 business day goes to Ann; in 2 business days, to Justin.

## Escalation process

Identify, classify priority, assess impact, attempt a safe resolution, escalate or get approval, own the next step, communicate, close. Mark a case Resolved when it is fixed and Closed only after confirmation and final documentation. Never put passwords or confidential personal data in the tracker.

### Priority levels

| Level | Trigger | Handling | Example |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **P1 Critical** | Severe outage, security incident, or major client or revenue exposure | Escalate immediately by direct communication; contain first | Multiple VAs can't access the system |
| **P2 High** | Material client disruption or significant deadline risk | Same-day management attention | Placement blocked before a client launch |
| **P3 Medium** | Limited disruption; a workaround is possible | Routine tracked escalation | Client workflow needs correction |
| **P4 Low** | Minor improvement or low-impact request | Scheduled review | Small dashboard improvement |

These response times are a proposed internal standard until Levrly leadership approves them.

### Who decides

> * **Team member:** reports the issue, documents impact, does safe troubleshooting.  
> * **Department lead:** checks severity, resolves within their authority, submits a recommendation.  
> * **Operations manager:** coordinates across departments, assigns an owner and a deadline.  
> * **Leadership:** decides exceptions on pricing, contracts, policy, refunds and other material impact. Every approval request carries a proposed resolution and the decision needed.  
> * **Assigned owner:** implements, updates the client, records the final resolution.

## Policies leadership set through escalations

Each rule cites the tracker case where it was decided.

> * **Signed and paid before handoff.** No client moves to Onboarding or Recruitment without a signed contract and first payment. Tara is automating sign-before-pay. Referral clients meet the OA team and onboard before sourcing; ESC-0032 was the last exception. (ESC-0001, 0006, 0012, 0027, 0032\)  
> * **All VA contracts are 12 months.** Month-to-month is an exception, not policy. Sales does not pitch custom plans outside the catalog, and any price override is documented in the contract notes. (ESC-0028)  
> * **Levrly does not guarantee VA performance.** Levrly recruits and trains. Payment dates are not moved because a VA started late. (ESC-0002)  
> * **VA terms are between the client and the VA.** Holiday, leave and similar addenda are client and VA agreements; Levrly can negotiate but can't make claims for a VA it doesn't employ. (ESC-0003)  
> * **Stripe billing.** Ongoing plans are monthly subscriptions, not one-time payments. Product names use a capital-L "Levrly" with no duplicates. Invoices have a 2-day due buffer. Check for a repeat charge the month after any refund. (ESC-0005, 0017, 0022, 0029\)  
> * **Back up every contract** in the master contracts folder in Google Drive. (ESC-0024)  
> * **Rates before alignment.** Recruitment flags candidates above the client's budget before alignment calls and sets rate expectations early for specialist roles. (ESC-0018, 0022, 0031\)  
> * **Clients outside the GHL agency** are supported through guidance and GHL email support; no transfer is required. (ESC-0015)  
> * **VA performance.** Client VAs get a training or transition plan first. Internal team VAs go on a 2-week evaluation, then offboarding if there is no improvement: notice sent, email suspended, GHL and OS access removed. (ESC-0013, 0016, 0019, 0026\)  
> * **Rejected candidates** who stay engaged are sent to courses and coaching so they can reapply. (ESC-0020)  
> * **Social platforms.** Keep human review on AI content and remove bots. (ESC-0033)
