# Client Lifecycle: Sales Handoff to Takeover

Steps 1 to 8 are the same for every plan. After the client approves the documents, the path splits: Foundation, Build and Scale go to Recruitment and the Client/VA handover; Launchpad runs Sprint and Milestone cycles. Total time to approved documents is about 5 to 7 business days.  
*Sources: Levrly OA – Internal Team Process; Levrly OA – The Onboarding & Blueprint Process; Levrly OA – The Launchpad Process; SOP: Onboarding and Blueprint Process.*

## Steps 1 to 8: every plan

| Step | Owner | Timing | What happens | Done when |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **1\. Sales to OA handoff** | Jye (Sales) | Day 0 | Lead entered in Levrly OS with the journey stage updated. Contract signed and first payment made. Handoff notes written using the Blueprint Prep Brief. Onboarding call booked 2 to 3 days after signing. | Ann or Haze reply "Accepted", or list what is missing, within 1 business day. |
| **2\. Account setup** | Ann / Haze | Day 0 to 1 | Levrly OS profile, client folder, row in the Ops Arch Active Clients tracker, GHL sub-account, Jared James Academy for real estate agents, OA section and deliverables. Foundation, Build and Scale: Wise setup through the referral link, OS access and courses, coaching calls. Launchpad: hours log opened at 20.0, addendum filed, Sprint and Milestone booking links sent. | Setup checklist ticked in OS. |
| **3\. Call booked and prep** | Ann / Haze | Before the call | Confirm the booking, or email the calendar link the same day. Collect the intake form and access list, run the access audit, prepare the plan deck, confirm OS and GHL invites are sent. | Call booked 2 to 3 days after signing; intake and access complete. |
| **4\. Onboarding & Blueprint call** | Ann, Haze or the Onboarding & Blueprint Specialist | 2 to 3 days after signing | Recording on before the call starts. Part 1: plan deck, VA role defined by daily tasks, tier, pay method, channel, client logged in to OS and GHL live. Part 2: red dot to blue dot. | Client is logged in, has confirmed the ranked bottlenecks and the one goal, and has committed. |
| **5\. Blueprint report** | Call owner to Ann | 1 business day after the call | Red dot findings and ranked bottlenecks, blue dot goal, requirements, recording link. Ann reviews the same day it arrives. | Ann approves it or returns a numbered gap list. |
| **6\. Plan email campaign** | Ann / Haze | Same day as the call | Activate the campaign for the client's plan and check that the first email sends. | Campaign is live. |
| **7\. Client documents** | Ann | 2 to 3 days after the call | 30/60/90 plan, Client Roadmap and Job Description, built with the "How to Create a Client Roadmap, 30/60/90 and JD" guide. Requested changes are made before any build or recruitment starts. | Client approves. |
| **8\. Internal framework and delegation** | Ann to task owners | Same day as approval | Roadmap broken into tasks, owners, hours and due dates (Scale 8 hours, Launchpad 20 hours). Task owners add deliverables in OS. | Every task has an owner, hours and a deadline. |

## Steps 9 to 12: Foundation, Build and Scale

| Step | Owner | What happens | Done when |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **9\. OA to Recruitment** | Ann / Haze to Kristine | Handoff email with the approved JD and client profile. | Kristine accepts within 1 business day. |
| **10\. Sourcing and alignment** | Kristine | 7 to 14 days of sourcing; 3 to 5 finalists; alignment interview within 3 days of the shortlist; client picks within 24 hours. | VA selected and handover booked. |
| **11\. Client/VA handover** | Ann or Haze | 45-minute Handover Deck; first bi-weekly client and VA calls booked. UNLOCK "Leverage" begins. | Start date and first calls confirmed before the VA starts. |
| **12\. Client takeover** | Ann and Haze | Client marked active in OS and the tracker; call cadence starts. | Marked active within 1 business day. |

## Steps 9 to 12: Launchpad

| Step | Owner | What happens | Done when |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **9\. Task delegation** | Ann | Each task delegated with hours, deadline and deliverable, inside 20 hours. | Every task has an owner and hours. |
| **10\. Sprint and Milestone cycles** | Task owners, Ann leads | One project per week. Build, test in the client's system, record a walkthrough, log every hour. Hours report at 50% and 75%. Raise remaining hours with the client at about 15 hours used. | Each milestone signed off and the next call booked. |
| **11\. Optimize and final handoff** | Ann or Haze | KPIs, feedback fixes, handover package. 30-minute final meeting: walk through the package, confirm owners, agree the next step, get sign-off, close in OS. At 20.0 hours, work stops. | Client signs off. |
| **12\. Exit to Sales or Recruitment** | Ann / Haze | Build or Scale upgrade goes to Jye. VA placement goes to Kristine and joins step 9 of the VA path. | Receiver accepts within 1 business day. |

## What the client gives us

> * Intake form and access list before the call.  
> * One decision-maker who approves the roadmap and priorities.  
> * Agreement and commitment at the Blueprint call.  
> * Replies within 2 business days by text, email or call.

## Quality check before any deliverable ships

> * Uses the SOP Master Template, Levrly branding and the official logo.  
> * Names a trigger, owner, tools, steps, output and end point.  
> * Tested once in the client's real system.  
> * Walkthrough video recorded and linked.  
> * Filed in the client folder, ticked in the OS checklist, hours logged against it.

## Launchpad handover package

> * Improvement report: before and after, bottlenecks fixed, hours used by deliverable.  
> * Updated roadmap: built, still open, recommended next 90 days.  
> * Every system built, with its walkthrough and location.  
> * KPIs and scorecard.  
> * Delegation strategy, VA readiness and ownership list.  
> * Access log.
