# Methods: OPTIMIZE, UNLOCK, Red Dot to Blue Dot

OPTIMIZE is how the OA team transforms the client's business. UNLOCK is how the team makes every placed VA succeed. Red dot to blue dot is the script used on every Blueprint call.  
*Sources: The OPTIMIZE Method – OA Team Internal Workflow; The UNLOCK Method – OA Team Delivery System; Onboarding & Blueprint Process.*

## OPTIMIZE (client side)

Target outcome: the client's business is systemized, measurable and ready to delegate. The OA team does not manage the client's business and is not VA labor.

| Move | Stage | What happens | Output |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **O: Observe** | Diagnose | Map workflows, tools, people and processes. For each workflow: trigger, steps, owner, decisions, end point. | Current-state process map |
| **P: Pinpoint** | Diagnose | Name bottlenecks, gaps, risks and dependencies out loud, ranked by time and money. The client confirms the list. | Ranked bottleneck list |
| **T: Transform** | Architect | Redesign what isn't working; map ownership, handoffs and decision rights. | Client roadmap and internal build plan |
| **I: Implement** | Implement | Build SOPs, automations, dashboards, templates and workflows in the client's tools. | Live systems with walkthroughs |
| **M: Measure** | Optimize | KPIs, reporting and accountability for what was built. | KPI baseline and scorecard |
| **I: Integrate** | Implement | Connect people, tools and communication. | Working handoffs between people and tools |
| **Z: Zero In** | Optimize | Refine the highest-impact areas from use and feedback. | Refined systems |
| **E: Expand** | Optimize | Prepare for delegation, hiring, automation and growth. | Delegation strategy and VA readiness |

### Launchpad week by week (20 hours)

| Week | Stage and hours | OA work | Signed off at Milestone Review |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | Diagnose, about 3 hours | Onboarding, access audit, Blueprint call, workflow maps, Blueprint report | Bottleneck list |
| 2 | Architect, about 4 hours | Roadmap and build plan; first build started | Roadmap and build priorities |
| 3 | Implement, about 10 hours | Top builds, tests, walkthroughs; hours report at 50% | Each deliverable shipped that week |
| 4 | Optimize, about 3 hours | Feedback fixes, KPIs, delegation strategy, VA readiness; hours report at 75% | Handover package and next step |

### Hours rules

> * 20 hours, logged in quarter-hours against a deliverable in Levrly OS. Meetings, building and documentation all count.  
> * The Lead Architect's internal reviews do not count against the client's hours.  
> * Scope changes are written into the internal build plan before work starts.  
> * If a task or feedback would run over, stop and get Ann's decision before logging more hours.  
> * At 20.0 hours, work stops. Anything left goes into the handoff recommendation.

### Four plan focuses

Pick the one that matches the client's biggest bottleneck.

> * **Marketing and Brand Operations:** the brand stays active and consistent without the owner approving every post.  
> * **Sales and Lead Management Operations:** no lead goes cold; follow-up lives in a system.  
> * **Executive and Business Operations:** admin load leaves the owner's calendar.  
> * **Team Growth and Recruitment Operations:** hiring and onboarding run to a documented standard.

## UNLOCK (VA side)

Target outcome: every placed VA shows up, stays consistent and acts without being told. U and N happen before the VA starts, L at handover, and O, C and K for as long as the VA is placed.

| Step | Owner | When | What happens |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **U: Understand** | Haze | After JD approval, before sourcing ends | Read the Blueprint report, roadmap and JD. Note the client's top 3 priorities, communication style and pet peeves. Write the role summary. |
| **N: Nail the Outcome** | Ann | Before the start date | One countable 90-day outcome. Working Structure: hours and time zone, pay rate and schedule (Wise), holidays and leave, meeting cadence, 3 to 5 KPIs, coaching cadence, channel and reply window. Confirmed with the client in writing. |
| **L: Leverage** | Ann and Haze | Handover, days 1 to 5 | Handover Deck; access checklist through a password manager; OS courses (Real Estate and GoHighLevel first, then the role course); first 3 SOPs; management tracker; Apploye working; all sessions booked. |
| **O: Own** | Haze | Daily for days 1 to 30, then weekly | Check the Daily Show-Up Standard; message any miss the same day and log it; weekly scorecard every Friday. |
| **C: Coach** | Ann and Haze | Weeks 2, 4, 6, 8 | Four 30-minute sessions; recap to the VA with the client copied within 24 hours. |
| **K: Keep Improving** | Ann | Day 30, 60, 90, then quarterly | Reviews; Day 90 and quarterly reviews are 45 minutes with the client joining for the last 15\. Improvement plan saved in OS. |

### Daily Show-Up Standard

> * Log in on time through the time tracker and greet the client in chat.  
> * Post the Start of Day report: priorities and blockers.  
> * Do the agreed check-ins and reply within the agreed window.  
> * Update the management tracker as work moves.  
> * Post the End of Day report: done, pending, blockers, next step.  
> * Log out through the time tracker.  
> * Attend every meeting and coaching call, or watch the recording and post one takeaway.  
> * Report any absence, delay or leave in advance through Levrly OS, never after.

### Weekly VA scorecard (Fridays, in Levrly OS)

> * On-time logins (time tracker)  
> * SOD and EOD posted  
> * Meetings and coaching attended  
> * Tasks closed by deadline  
> * Issues or ideas raised by the VA first  
> * Times the client chased the VA (target: zero)

### Bi-weekly coaching sessions

| Session | Week | Focus | Leave with |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | 2 | Outcome check, SOD/EOD routine, tool questions | VA can repeat the 90-day outcome; access gaps closed |
| 2 | 4 | Which SOPs and tools are used daily; where time is lost | One SOP to write or improve |
| 3 | 6 | What the VA did without being asked; deadlines; escalation | Two tasks the VA fully owns |
| 4 | 8 | Client joins; 60-day review | Next-30-day priorities |

## Red dot to blue dot

### Red dot: where the business is today

Ask: "Walk me through the last time you \_\_\_, start to finish. What did you do first?" Then ask where it slowed down, what only the client knows, where it is written down, and what access the VA would need. Leave with the workflow, where it breaks and what it costs, in the client's words. Done when the client says "that's right" to your recap.

### Blue dot: where the client wants to be

Foundation, Build and Scale: "What do you need your VA to have achieved 90 days from now?" Take day 30, 60 and 90 one at a time. Launchpad: "In 30 days, what is one thing that should be working that isn't today?" Write the goal as from \[A\] to \[B\] by \[date\], with today's number and the target number filled in live.

### Script rules

> * Red dot first. Map one real, recent example and suggest nothing yet. If the client starts suggesting fixes: "Hold that thought, I'm writing it down."  
> * One blue dot. Three goals means zero goals.  
> * Both dots get a number: "What number would we check on Friday?"  
> * Bring vague answers back: "Let's take the most recent one. What actually happened?"
