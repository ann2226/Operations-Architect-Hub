## SOPs

### Reviewing Sales Handoff Notes

**Owner:** Ann (Haze covers). **Trigger:** Sales sends the handoff email after the client signs (Day 0). **Deadline:** accept or flag within 1 business day. [Open the SOP](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1wM1HQmpEZ5j02NUSKequcC44-EPj-7zC/view)

> 1. Open the package the same day: handoff email with notes, signed contract, plan confirmed, call booked 2 to 3 days out, lead in OS with the journey stage updated. If anything is missing, flag Sales right away.  
> 2. Check the notes against the 8 sections of the Blueprint Prep Brief: the short version, how we got here, the people, the company, red and blue dots, the placements, what Sales told the client, the session. If 2 or more sections are missing, return to Sales.  
> 3. Verify facts and commitments: every number traces to a call, email or proposal; terms match the standard; unknowns are labeled "Not defined yet". A commitment Levrly can't honor goes to Sales and Justin before the call.  
> 4. Reply "Accepted" or send a numbered gap list, never both. Add open questions to the call prep.  
> 5. File the notes and contract in OS, update the OA section, start account setup.

### Client Onboarding & Blueprint Process

**Owners:** Jye, Ann, Haze, the Onboarding & Blueprint Specialist. **Trigger:** client signs. **Time:** about 5 to 7 business days. This is steps 1 to 8 of the Client Lifecycle document. [Open the SOP](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1pfHGMD_lsL9Zoi_nKRt5fVcAAfClWm0d/view)  
**Part 1, plan deck:** announce the working session (slide 2); plan in one or two sentences (3); define the VA role by daily tasks and tools (4 to 5); VA tier (6); screening, top 3 to 5 candidates, client decides within 24 hours of alignment (7 to 9); pay method (10); VA channel and coaching (11 to 13); client logs in to OS and GHL on their own device (14 to 16). Don't move on until the client sees the dashboard.  
**Part 2:** red dot to blue dot. If the client won't commit, Ann joins the follow-up. Never quote upgrade or add-on prices on the call.

### Reviewing Onboarding & Blueprint Calls

**Owner:** Ann. **Trigger:** the Blueprint report arrives. **Deadline:** decide the same day. [Open the SOP](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1SJq27u_Tfiq8wx96ZeSZNMhJpo7xKOMw/view)

> 1. Confirm the report, recording link and live email campaign arrived.  
> 2. Review the onboarding part: tasks, not titles; tools; tier; pay method; channel; client logged in; no prices quoted.  
> 3. Review the red dot: one real recent workflow mapped end to end, bottlenecks ranked by cost, in the client's words, confirmed by the client.  
> 4. Review the blue dot: one goal from A to B by a date, with both numbers; client committed.  
> 5. Cross-check the handoff notes: open questions answered; no conflict with what Sales promised.  
> 6. Approve and start the client documents, or return a numbered gap list tied to the failed checks with an update-by date. Note coaching points.

### OA Pre-Handover Checklist and Client Questions

**Owner:** Operations Architect. **When:** before taking a client and before any design. [Open the checklist](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1rtl3-SgQlZtz3oR49DE-akqA4WX2-4UI/view)

> * **B.** Problem statement states reasons, not service names.  
> * **C.** Each outcome traces to a pain point and is measurable.  
> * **D.** Systems inventory lists current system, primary use, known issue and VA access needed.

Three client call stages: Blueprint/Architecture call, Systems and Tools Audit (screen-shared), Solution Design Sign-Off (explicit approval; nothing agreed only verbally).

## Call scripts

Rules for every script: only Sales quotes the price; the client commits on the call; every call ends with a task and a date for both sides and the next call booked; say "our team", not "I"; scope requests and upgrade signals go to Sales the same day.

### Foundation

First VA hire, \$499 a month (quoted only by Sales). 28 slides, 90-day goal. After setting Point A to Point B, list every task and give each line one name: client (decisions and access), VA (daily work) or us (tools and training). Hand off to Recruitment the same day with tasks, tools, hours, tier, the 90-day goal and any concerns. [Open the script](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Yasm4ZBPuLHFzgm72UUHe2uNp-bVV4tR/view)

### Build

One VA plus a monthly 2-hour Architect session (1 hour client, 1 hour VA). One goal and two weekly habits; the VA owns at least one. Monthly session: 5 min win, 10 scoreboard, 15 red dot, 75 build with SOP and walkthrough, 10 hand to the VA, 5 next steps. A 20-hour Architect block can be added for a bigger build. [Open the script](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1CQFEPXj0kLBAEvsKmeQrPxZkWULzRSJy/view)

### Scale

Two VAs plus 2 Architect hours every week (about 8 a month). One goal, two habits, each VA owns a habit. Weekly: 15-minute check-in (Report, Review, Commit, Reset), 90 minutes building, 10 minutes handing to the owning VA, 5 minutes on next steps. Bigger reviews at 30/60/90 days, after each quarterly planning with Joel and Justin, and at month 9 (flag Year Two to Sales). [Open the script](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1r5tK739iiSOwxk__OEHgSPheSE3E3aW3/view)

### Launchpad

20 OA hours over 30 days on one project. 17 slides. The goal sentence is drafted from the Sales notes before the call. Ask for three commitments: access this week, approval in week 2, 15 minutes a week. Raise remaining hours at about 15 hours used, with three paths: keep working with the team, hire a VA, or finish it themselves. [Open the script](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1TixmrAoNwSiEr-z3Gbjt06NDKg5i84q8/view)

### VA pay tiers (all VA plans)

Entry \$4 to \$6 an hour, Experienced \$6 to \$10, Senior \$10 to \$15 or more. Pay goes directly from the client to the VA, hourly or as a fixed retainer, through Wise, PayPal, Payoneer or GCash.

### Common objections

| If the client says | Say | Owner |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Starts suggesting fixes | "Hold that thought, I'm writing it down." | OA |
| "Everything is broken." | "Which one, if fixed, would make the rest easier?" | OA |
| "I don't know the number." | "Best guess; we measure it in week 1." | OA |
| "I don't want to give access." | "What worries you? Your VA sees only what they need." | OA |
| Takes work back from the VA | "Was it the result, or how it was done?" | OA |
| Misses check-ins | "What would make these 15 minutes worth your time?" | OA |
| "Can you also build \_\_\_?" or the VA is full | "Let me set up time with \[sales rep\]." Log it as an upgrade signal. | OA to Sales |
