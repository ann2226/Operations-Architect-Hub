# Meetings and Cadence

Client and consultation calls go between 10:30 AM and 4:00 PM. No 9:00 AM calls unless urgent and approved. Every call ends with the next call booked and a report from whoever ran it. Send one updated invite when a time changes, never duplicates. Block time off in the calendar so invites auto-decline.  
*Sources: OA Milestone Meetings SOP v2.0; OA Sprint Meeting SOP v2.0; Internal Team Process; UNLOCK Method; call scripts.*

## Milestone meeting agenda (client-facing, 10 to 15 minutes)

> 1. Welcome and purpose (1 min).  
> 2. Last week's accomplishments, with where each deliverable lives in Levrly OS (3 to 4 min).  
> 3. This week's priorities and delivery-map phase: Mapping, Design, Build, Handover (3 to 4 min).  
> 4. Client action items: access shared through a password manager, brand assets, copy, approvals (2 min).  
> 5. Upcoming milestones, scoreboard progress, timeline changes (1 to 2 min).  
> 6. Questions and confirm the next meeting (1 min).

**Same day after the meeting:** update the Milestone Tracker and Project Scoreboard, upload deliverables to the OA section, save the Gemini notes, follow up in the client's preferred channel, add tasks to the client's Google Chat task board, notify the team, send the weekly report and book the next meeting. If access is still missing, follow up daily by email and text until it is granted.  
**Weekly report:** with Sprint meetings only, send it every Friday. With Milestone meetings only, send it every Monday.

## OA Team Sprint Meeting agenda (internal, 15 to 30 minutes)

> 1. Welcome, objectives and announcements (2 to 3 min).  
> 2. Team status: each OA's end-of-week goals with completion percentage, wins, lessons (5 min).  
> 3. Active client review: phase, milestones, deadlines, approvals, pending access, risks; one weekly goal per client (8 to 10 min).  
> 4. Placed VA check: scorecards, misses against the Daily Show-Up Standard, reviews due, any VA at the second or third miss (3 to 5 min).  
> 5. New clients, onboardings and handovers: who schedules each one (3 to 5 min).  
> 6. Sprint planning: owner, priority and due date for every task, and what is deprioritized this week (8 to 10 min).  
> 7. Schedule and coverage (2 to 3 min).  
> 8. Risks, blockers and dependencies, each with a follow-up owner (3 to 5 min).  
> 9. Wrap-up: decisions, goals, actions; remind everyone to update the tracker and OS (1 to 2 min).

Client conversations go in each client's Google Chat space, not the main team space. Branded reports and roadmaps follow the Levrly brand guide.

## Escalate to Ann from any meeting

> * Client repeatedly misses meetings, is dissatisfied, asks for out-of-scope work, contract changes, or raises billing concerns.  
> * A delay of more than 1 week, or access still missing after daily follow-ups.  
> * Technical or third-party limits, workload over capacity, or a need for another department or specialist.

Include the client, an issue summary, the timeline impact, follow-up dates, and links to the tracker, OS record or Gemini notes.
