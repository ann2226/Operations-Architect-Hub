Method · client side

The OPTIMIZE method

How the OA team transforms the client's business: four stages, eight moves. Target outcome: the business is systemized, measurable and ready to delegate. The OA team is not VA labor and does not manage the client's business.

O

Observe · Diagnose

Map workflows, tools, people: trigger, steps, owner, decisions, end point. → Current-state process map

P

Pinpoint · Diagnose

Name bottlenecks, gaps, risks out loud, ranked by time/money. Client confirms. → Ranked bottleneck list

T

Transform · Architect

Redesign; map ownership, handoffs, decision rights. → Roadmap + internal build plan

I

Implement · Implement

Build SOPs, automations, dashboards, templates in the client's tools. → Live systems + walkthroughs

M

Measure · Optimize

KPIs, reporting, accountability. → KPI baseline and scorecard

I

Integrate · Implement

Connect people, tools and communication. → Working hand-offs

Z

Zero In · Optimize

Refine highest-impact areas from use and feedback. → Refined systems

E

Expand · Optimize

Prepare delegation, hiring, growth. → Delegation strategy + VA readiness

Method · VA side

The UNLOCK VA method

Target outcome: every placed VA shows up, stays consistent and acts without being told. U and N happen before the VA starts, L at handover, and O, C and K for as long as the VA is placed.

U

Understand

Haze · after JD approval. Read Blueprint report, roadmap, JD; client's top 3 priorities, style, pet peeves; role summary.

N

Nail the Outcome

Ann · before start. One countable 90-day outcome + Working Structure (hours, time zone, pay via Wise, leave, 3–5 KPIs, channel, reply window). Confirmed with client in writing.

L

Leverage

Ann & Haze · Days 1–5. Handover Deck, access via password manager, OS courses (Real Estate + GHL first), first 3 SOPs, management tracker, Apploye working.

O

Own

Haze · daily Days 1–30, then weekly. Check the Daily Show-Up Standard, message misses same day, scorecard every Friday.

C

Coach

Ann & Haze · Weeks 2, 4, 6, 8. Four 30-min sessions; recap to VA (client copied) within 24 h.

K

Keep Improving

Ann · Day 30, 60, 90, then quarterly. Day 90/quarterly reviews are 45 min, client joins last 15. Improvement plan saved in OS.

Method · on every Blueprint call

Red dot → blue dot

Red dot: where the business is today

Ask: “Walk me through the last time you ___, start to finish. What did you do first?” Then: where it slowed down, what only the client knows, where it's written down, what access the VA needs.

Be the therapist, not the fixer. Done when the client says “that's right” to your recap.

Blue dot: where they want to be

VA plans: “What do you need your VA to have achieved 90 days from now?” Take day 30, 60, 90 one at a time. Launchpad: “In 30 days, what is one thing that should be working that isn't today?”

Write it as from [A] to [B] by [date], with today's number and the target filled in live.

Three rules. Red dot before blue dot (“Hold that thought, I'm writing it down”). One blue dot: three goals means zero goals. Both dots get a number: “What number would we check on Friday?” Bring vague answers back: “Last Tuesday, what actually happened?”

Reviewing Sales Handoff Notes

Ann (Haze covers)Trigger: handoff email, Day 0Accept or flag in 1 business day

Catch gaps before the Blueprint call so the client never repeats themselves.
Steps
  1. Open the package (same day): handoff email with notes, signed contract, plan confirmed, call booked 2–3 days out, lead in OS with journey stage. Missing item → flag Sales right away.
  2. Check notes against the 8-section Blueprint Prep Brief: short version, how we got here, people, company, red/blue dots, placements, what Sales told the client, the session. 2+ sections missing → return to Sales.
  3. Verify facts and commitments: every number traces to a call, email or proposal; terms match standard; unknowns labeled “Not defined yet”; every attendee has an email. Commitment Levrly can't honor → Sales and Justin before the call.
  4. Accept or flag: reply “Accepted” or a numbered gap list, never both. Add open questions to call prep.
  5. File and start setup (Day 0–1): notes and contract in OS, update OA section, start account setup.
Rules

Don't fill gaps yourself; send them back to Sales. Honor Sales' commitments; resolve unworkable ones with Sales and Justin before the client hears otherwise. Gaps unresolved at 2 business days → Justin.

Control · Escalation Tracker

Escalation playbook

Log each client or operational issue once in the Levrly Escalation Tracker, with a recommendation, one owner and a current next step. Never enter passwords or confidential personal data in the tracker.

Process: Identify → Classify priority → Assess impact → Attempt safe resolution → Escalate / approve → Own next step → Communicate → Close. Mark Resolved when fixed; Closed only after confirmation and final documentation.
LevelTriggerHandlingExample
P1 · CriticalSevere outage, security incident, major client or revenue exposureEscalate immediately by direct communication; contain firstMultiple VAs can't access the system
P2 · HighMaterial client disruption or significant deadline riskSame-day management attentionPlacement blocked before a client launch
P3 · MediumLimited disruption; workaround possibleRoutine tracked escalationClient workflow needs correction
P4 · LowMinor improvement or low-impact requestScheduled reviewSmall dashboard improvement

Priority response times are a proposed internal standard until Levrly leadership approves them.

Who decides

  • Team member: reports, documents impact, safe troubleshooting
  • Department lead: checks severity, resolves within authority, recommends
  • Operations manager: coordinates, assigns owner and deadline
  • Leadership: pricing, contracts, policy, refunds, material exceptions
  • Assigned owner: implements, updates client, records resolution

Leadership rhythm

  1. Prioritize P1/P2 first
  2. Decide: every approval request carries a proposed resolution and the decision needed
  3. Assign: one owner, a next action, a target date
  4. Communicate: set the next client update date
  5. Close: Resolved, then Closed after confirmation

Levrly recruits and trains. Don't move payment dates because a VA started late, and don't make it a habit.

ESC-0002

VA terms are between client and VA

Holiday, leave or similar addenda are agreements between the client and the VA. Levrly can negotiate but can't make claims for a VA it doesn't employ.

ESC-0003

Stripe billing setup

Ongoing plans are monthly subscriptions, not one-time payments. Product names use capital-L “Levrly”; no duplicates. 2-day invoice due buffer. Watch for double charges the month after a refund.

ESC-0005001700220029

Back up every contract

All client and VA contracts are saved in the master backup folder in Google Drive, not only in Levrly OS.

ESC-0024

Rates and budgets before alignment

Recruitment flags candidates above the client's budget before alignment calls, and sets rate expectations with the client early for specialist roles.

ESC-001800220031

Clients outside the GHL agency

Clients who keep their own GHL account are supported through guidance and GHL email support; a transfer isn't required.

ESC-0015

VA performance: coach first, then evaluate

Client VAs get a training or transition plan first (TC, GHL, compliance coaching). Internal team VAs go on a 2-week evaluation, then offboarding: notice, email suspended, GHL/OS access removed.

ESC-0013001600190026

Rejected candidates can reapply

Send repeatedly rejected but engaged candidates to courses and coaching so they can reapply.

ESC-0020

Automation on social platforms

Keep human review on AI content and remove bots. Ann proposed quarterly research meetings on Meta platform rules.
